Scheduled for 06:30 Eastern, weekday mornings.

Every weekday this page is rebuilt with new arXiv preprints relevant to the ASME IDETC-CIE Systems Engineering, Information and Knowledge Management (SEIKM) technical committee, sorted into the topic areas below. Inclusion is not endorsement — these are unreviewed preprints selected by keyword matching, not by a person.

Topic areas

Following the SEIKM 2027 track proposal. The phrase lists behind each one live in config/topics.yaml and are a one-line pull request to change.

SEIKM-01Systems Engineering and Complex SystemsMBSE, system architecture, decomposition, integration and system analysis, including research that does not use AI.
SEIKM-02Informatics for Design and ManufacturingEngineering information and knowledge management, interoperability, LLMs and agent architectures.
SEIKM-03Design, Simulation and Optimization for Advanced ManufacturingDesign, simulation and optimization across advanced manufacturing, explicitly retaining additive manufacturing. Joint topic with AMS.
SEIKM-04Digital Twins, Manufacturing Systems, and Supply ChainsDesign-to-operation models, production planning, scheduling and supply-chain decisions. Manufacturing and supply-chain work does not need to use a digital twin.
SEIKM-05Engineering Knowledge and Physics-Informed AI/MLJoint topic with AI/ML. Physics-informed and knowledge-guided learning for engineering, including scientific machine learning.
SEIKM-06Systems DesignProposed joint topic with DTM. Design methods and early design decisions connected to requirements, system architectures, MBSE, trade-off analysis and engineering knowledge.
SEIKM-07Emerging Topics in SEIKMHuman-AI teaming, robotics and physical AI. Deployability, reproducibility and reuse; cyber-physical-social systems.
SEIKM-GENSEIKM GeneralFundamental and applied SEIKM research outside the named topics, including work that may suggest future topic areas.

Follow along

Subscribe by RSS, or watch the repository on GitHub for a notification each morning. Past issues collect in the archive.

How papers get here. Every weekday a job queries arXiv across the categories and phrases listed in config/settings.yaml, drops anything that fails an engineering-relevance gate, and sorts the rest into the SEIKM topic areas using the weighted phrase lists in config/topics.yaml. Nothing is hand-picked, and nothing is peer-reviewed by this site — inclusion is not endorsement.

Topic definitions follow the SEIKM 2027 track proposal. To correct a mis-filed paper or widen a topic, edit config/topics.yaml and open a pull request — no code changes needed.
